About

Get to know the developer behind the product—how I think, build, and solve problems.

Empowering ideas through thoughtful engineering and practical problem-solving.

Hi, I’m Eunijoy a self-taught full-stack developer who builds with purpose.

I’m a self-taught full-stack developer who started learning programming in the public library in Naga City, spending every free hour studying and building. I didn’t excel academically in high school, but I discovered early on that I had a deep interest in programming and that curiosity changed my direction completely.

Instead of going to college, I chose to commit fully to learning through self-study and real-world practice. At 18, I began freelancing, taking on small projects to sharpen my skills and support my family financially. What started as survival became a long-term pursuit of mastery.

I chose software development not only out of passion, but with a clear purpose: to build a better future for my family. There were moments when I almost gave up, but I realized growth requires discipline, resilience, and consistency.

Today, I continue to build and improve not just for myself, but as an example to my two younger brothers who are now pursuing careers in game development and web development. My journey has taught me that persistence, hard work, and self-belief matter more than perfect grades.
